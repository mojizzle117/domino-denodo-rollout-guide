APPLICATION DATA AND REPORT STORAGE PILOT

The options assessment covers application data, report provenance, snapshots and
generated files. The storage decision and synthetic proof are pending.

Inputs to the decision

1. One reporting use, including the input owner, record meaning, format, update
   and correction rules, and permitted access and retention.
2. The storage services and clients supported by the platform, including durable
   mounts, service identities and backup and restore support.
3. The apps and jobs that write, including how often they write, expected overlap,
   retry and conflict rules, and who owns recovery.

Acceptance

The first synthetic proof should save and reopen the intended input version,
link one generated file to a completed analytical run and exercise validation,
retries or edit conflicts, restart and isolated restore. Use the results to
decide whether the proposed storage fits the workload and operating requirements.

The companion storage options note describes the approach and links to the
SQLite and Domino documentation. Platform capabilities and runtime behavior
remain to be verified in the target environment.
